MEET 4WAYS

Real estate with purpose.

Founded by Jimmie Winbush Sr., 4Ways Property Group invests in residential real estate throughout Central Indiana. Our focus is acquiring properties with potential, completing thoughtful improvements, and creating long-term value for residents, communities, partners, and future generations.

We approach every opportunity with straightforward communication, responsible underwriting, and respect for the people connected to the property.
